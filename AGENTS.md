# Steel Blitz

Steel Blitz is an exploration of 3D game engine architecture across multiple platforms. It will be a simple arcade-style tank game written in C++, initially targeting OpenGL and the PlayStation Portable (PSP).

## Core Gameplay

Players battle enemy tanks in compact 3D arenas designed for short, fast-paced sessions lasting approximately 2–4 minutes.

The core gameplay loop is:

1. Enter an arena containing enemy tanks, obstacles, and cover.
2. Drive and maneuver with responsive arcade-style tank controls.
3. Aim and shoot from either a third-person driving view or a first-person gunner view.
4. Destroy enemies while avoiding projectiles and managing health.
5. Collect health, temporary shields, and weapon upgrades.
6. Clear the arena and advance to a more challenging level.

## Core Mechanics

- Third-person driving and first-person aiming cameras.
- Visible cannon shells, explosions, and basic splash damage.
- Enemy tanks that patrol, chase, shoot, and maneuver around obstacles.
- Increasing difficulty through more enemies, varied tank types, and more complex arenas.
- Quick, satisfying combat with simple controls, exaggerated action, and minimal realism.
